Lead Form Experience Roadmap
============================

1. Warm Onboarding (First 10 Seconds)
   - Introduce a hero question with celebratory microcopy and progress pulse.
   - Pre-fill/auto-detect basics where possible; defer optional fields.

2. Narrative Chapters
   - Split the form into themed chapters with a stepper showing time per stage.
   - Ensure transitions feel like mini milestones.

3. Real-time Personalisation
   - Surface contextual insights next to inputs (e.g., relevant tracks or scholarship hints).
   - Display dynamic progress stats and motivational nudges.

4. Micro-interactions & Flow
   - Replace heavy fields with chips/sliders and animation on step completion.
   - Keep navigation snappy; avoid dead-air loading states.

5. Trust & Privacy Reassurance
   - Attach in-context privacy notes and a persistent reassurance badge.
   - Offer transparent usage explanations for sensitive data requests.

6. Guidance & Safety Net
   - Provide smart defaults plus a “finish with counsellor” escape hatch.
   - Enable save/resume and quick contact options.

7. Immediate Payoff
   - Present a personalised results panel post-submit with actionable next steps.
   - Encourage continuing journey (call booking, WhatsApp handoff, downloads).

8. Instrumentation & Iteration
   - Track step-level drop-off, run A/B tests, and create a tweak backlog.
   - Feed insights back into analytics dashboards for continuous improvement.
